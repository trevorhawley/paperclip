# Paperclip north star (Trevor's fork)

Written 2026-11-26 from Trevor's own description. This is the "why". [plans/2026-11-26-roadmap.md](plans/2026-11-26-roadmap.md) is the "what next".

## The one-line version

**One chat front door to all my AI work, and it feels as good as working with a Claude project coordinator.**

## Who it is for

Trevor is the central person many people look to for fulfilment. He coordinates many projects, people and ideas: SEO and marketing clients, courses and businesses. He needs **one point of contact with AI** that organizes everything for him, instead of a dozen tools and chats.

## How it should work

1. **One front door.** Trevor talks to one chat, often loosely or by voice. It works out what he means.
2. **Cheap model for most of it.** A low-cost model (for example GPT Instant through the ChatGPT app) handles 80%+ of top-level chat, insight and system management at near-zero token cost.
3. **Coordinator, not doer.** For deeper work the front door spins off organized threads or projects and hands each to the best-fit model or agent (Claude, Codex or others). Agents work together, and their results flow back as short updates.
4. **Mirrors his real systems.** Work stays in step with ClickUp (tasks), Google Docs (course and framework masters) and his other tools, so nothing lives in two places.
5. **Orchestration, not deep-dive coding.** Paperclip is for running SEO, marketing and business projects. Software building stays in Claude Code.

## What "feels like this" means

The reference is a Claude project: a coordinator chat with threads. Trevor called it "hard to beat". It isn't the model alone. It's:

- **Short, outcome-first replies.** The coordinator acts first and asks rarely, with at most one or two questions.
- **Instant acknowledgement.** A reaction or one line right away, then the result when it's ready.
- **Visible delegation.** Work branches into named threads with live checklists, and each reports back.
- **Event-driven.** The chat speaks up when a thread finishes or needs him, not only when he types.
- **Memory.** It knows his preferences and projects every turn and never re-asks.
- **Decision cards.** Choices come as buttons with a recommendation.
- **Compact, glanceable UI.** See [FORK-UI-DENSITY.md](FORK-UI-DENSITY.md): every row says what it is, why it matters, and what he gets from it.

UX plus performance is the product value.
